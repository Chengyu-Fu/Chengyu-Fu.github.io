abstract: >-
 Accurate springback radius measurement and prediction are essential for springback compensation and forming accuracy control in tube free bending. However, conventional contact measurement methods and existing springback prediction models face challenges in balancing accuracy and efficiency. Therefore, this study proposes a metrologically characterized springback radius measurement method, and integrates analytical modeling, finite element (FE) simulation, and machine learning techniques for springback radius prediction. First, the fitted circular radius of the centerline in the constant-curvature region after unloading is defined as the measurand. A measurement chain integrating 3D scanning, point-cloud processing, centerline extraction, and two-stage circle fitting is established. The impact of coordinate perturbations on the fitted radius is quantified using a radius error amplification factor, and a complete measurement uncertainty budget is developed. For Al6061 aluminum alloy tubes, the proposed method achieves an expanded uncertainty of 0.86 mm (k = 2) and a repeatability standard deviation of 0.63 mm. Furthermore, a hybrid WOA-Attention-BP prediction framework is developed, where WOA optimizes network parameters and attention mechanism adaptively weights input features. The model achieves prediction performance with MAE, RMSE, and MAPE of 3.13 mm, 3.52 mm, and 2.09%, respectively, outperforming other mainstream forecasting models in accuracy. Furthermore, by integrating measurement uncertainty into the prediction residuals, the model prediction uncertainty is determined to be 3.67 mm. This study provides a reliable theoretical foundation for rapid springback prediction in free bending and applications in intelligent manufacturing.

  abstract: >-
    准确测量和预测回弹半径对于管材自由弯曲的回弹补偿和成形精度控制至关重要。然而，传统接触式测量方法与现有回弹预测模型难以兼顾精度和效率。为此，本研究提出一种具有计量学表征的回弹半径测量方法，并结合解析建模、有限元仿真和机器学习预测回弹半径。首先，将卸载后等曲率区段的中心线拟合圆半径定义为被测量，建立由三维扫描、点云处理、中心线提取和两阶段圆拟合组成的测量链。随后，利用半径误差放大因子量化坐标扰动对拟合半径的影响，并建立完整的测量不确定度预算。对 Al6061 铝合金管材，该方法的扩展不确定度为 0.86 mm（k = 2），重复性标准差为 0.63 mm。进一步构建 WOA-Attention-BP 混合预测框架，利用鲸鱼优化算法优化网络参数，并以注意力机制自适应地分配输入特征权重。模型的 MAE、RMSE 和 MAPE 分别为 3.13 mm、3.52 mm 和 2.09%，预测精度优于其他主流模型。将测量不确定度纳入预测残差后，模型预测不确定度为 3.67 mm。本研究为管材自由弯曲回弹的快速预测及其在智能制造中的应用提供了理论依据。
